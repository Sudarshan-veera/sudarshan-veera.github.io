Notes

Taping out a SKY130 ASIC as a student

The TinyTapeout submission meant working within real silicon constraints rather than simulation-only limits — an 80% tile utilization ceiling, a strict power budget, and gate-level checks that don't forgive shortcuts. The most instructive part was debugging forbidden met5 PDN violations and cocotb X-value errors: each fix required tracing the issue back through the physical design flow in OpenROAD rather than just the RTL. It reframed hardware design as a negotiation with the fabrication process, not just the logic.
